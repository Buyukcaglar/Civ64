# DOS recovery: open questions (extension D0, prompt 6 items 5 and 7)

Draft of 2026-10-11, revised the same day by fixer F3 after review R3 and the items fixers F1
and F2 passed on from reviews R1 and R2 (`docs/verify/ext-d0-fixes-r3.md`). The register of what the D0 drafts leave unknown or
undecided. Each entry names the question, the file and Open item that holds the detail, and what
it affects. Rule 6.7 applies to every entry: an unknown stays unknown; the profile shows the
nearest proven DOS behaviour, or the Windows behaviour, with a register entry, and never guesses.

Groups:
* **P: decisions the profile's design needs** (D1 resolves them, by a recorded choice or by
  evidence; the facts the decision needs are in the entry or its Detail);
* **O: answerable by a DOS-oracle observation** (the grant exists, 2.1; nothing was observed in
  D0);
* **L: listing work left, needed by a later phase** (named);
* **X: outside the profile's needs** (kept for completeness; no phase depends on them).

Entries are sorted by id; ids are stable. Abbreviations: SC = SCENES.md, SP = SCENES-PLAY.md,
WI = WINDOWS-INVENTORY.md, CORR = CORRESPONDENCE.md, PROF = `../DOS-PROFILE.md`.
